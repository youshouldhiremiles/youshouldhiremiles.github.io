GLASS CANNON — MILES FROM HOME STUDIOS

Open index.html in a modern browser. Keep assets and audio folders alongside it. The portfolio edition is also compatible with free GitHub Pages hosting.

PLAY
Enter the dungeon starts a run. A/D or left/right arrows move sideways. W/S or up/down arrows move within a limited forward/back band. Hold Space to cast. F unleashes a charged fireball. P or Pause pauses. Restart returns to the branded title screen. Touch: drag to move and cast, or use the six touch buttons.

AUDIO
Music and Sound effects are separate toggles and both default to on. Music volume affects only the soundtrack. Muting effects immediately silences ongoing effects without stopping the music. Music starts after entering the dungeon and pauses with the game or when the page is hidden. All three tracks use the vintage computer-speaker treatment; see MUSIC-CREDITS.txt for sources, licenses, and modifications.

GAMEPLAY
Mixed enemies enter on varied paths, assemble into one of four formations, and dive toward the wizard. Survivors return to their formation slots. Goblins and armor cause contact damage; skeletons fire arrows; evil wizards fire spells. Ghost contact drains fireball charge without costing a life.
Blue S potions grant spread shot; green B potions grant bounce shot; red F potions grant short-range flame. Effects last 14 seconds. Only evil wizards randomly drop potions; bosses can drop one when first falling below half health. At most two potions are active at once.
A boss appears every fifth stage, rotating through all five enemy archetypes. Boss health equals its stage number. Each ordinary hit deals one damage; a charged fireball deals half maximum boss health rounded up. Death preserves the fight and boss health, with three seconds of respawn protection. Stage clears remove all projectiles.

RELEASE INTERFACE
Practice, preview, and cheat buttons have been removed. Only Pause, Restart, normal touch controls, and audio controls remain. Automated gameplay and independent-audio-toggle checks passed. Browser inspection confirmed the cleaned interface and muting effects while music remains enabled.
