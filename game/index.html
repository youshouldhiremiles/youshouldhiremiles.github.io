Glass Cannon

“I didn’t ask how large the room is.”

Music and effects begin when you enter

How to play

Break the formation, dodge incoming attacks, and clear each dungeon wave.

Move
← → or A / D
↑ ↓ or W / S for limited forward/back movement
Cast
Hold Space
Fireball
F when the gold meter is full
Pause
P or the Pause button

Potions

Red F · Short-range flame
Blue S · Spread shot
Green B · Bounce shot

Potions last 14 seconds. Evil wizards may drop them; each boss drops one when first weakened below half health. Bosses appear every fifth wave. On touch screens, drag across the dungeon to move and cast.

Music is on. Enter the dungeon to begin.
